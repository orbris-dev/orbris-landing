Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

Orbris LLC ("we") sells Chunova mattresses on Amazon and TikTok Shop. This site, orbris.com, is a public page that links to those stores.

What this site collects

This site has no accounts, no forms and no ads. We do not set cookies on this site. If you email us, we get your email address and your message.

Orders

When you buy on Amazon or TikTok Shop, the store collects your order details. We use them only to fill your order, handle returns and give support. Each store has its own privacy policy.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share order details only with the store, the shipper and the tools needed to fill your order.
